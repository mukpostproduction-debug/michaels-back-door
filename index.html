44 Years of Excellence
Heritage & Passion

Four Decades of Italian Hospitality

Established in 1981 by Michael Morra Sr., Michael’s Back Door Restaurant has been providing Mississauga and the Greater Toronto Area with award-winning Italian cuisine and professional courteous service in a warm, comfortable atmosphere for 44 continuous years.

Principal owner Michael Morra Jr., along with partner and Executive Chef Mark D’Souza, invite you to experience creative Italian and continental fare, featuring the freshest scratch ingredients and an extensive cellar of rare vintages.

Head Chef Mark D’Souza joined Michael’s Back Door in 2015. Since taking the helm of the kitchen, Chef Mark and his brigade have focused on adding a modern twist to traditional rustic regional Italian dishes, from hand-shaped agnolotti to our legendary lobster angel hair.

Whether you are looking for a casual family dinner, a romantic anniversary in Lover’s Lane, or a milestone gathering under the stars in the Solarium, Michael’s Back Door has the perfect table waiting for you.

How to Find the Back Door

1715 Lakeshore Road West, Mississauga, ON L5J 1J4

Rear Alley Parking Directions:

  1. From Lakeshore Road West, turn north onto Clarkson Road North.
  2. Within 30 meters, turn into the immediate alleyway entrance behind the commercial buildings.
  3. Park in our private dedicated guest lot directly behind the restaurant.
  4. Look for the iconic dark walnut door under the glowing amber lantern.
